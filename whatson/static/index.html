Where the guide and library come from. WhatsOn reads one server at a time, and Plezy has to be signed in to the same one on each TV.

Plexin use

WhatsOn reads your Plex server's Live TV guide and library. Sign in to choose a server.

Paste a token instead

In Plex Web, open any movie, choose Get Info → View XML, and copy the X-Plex-Token value from the address bar. A pasted token keeps working until you sign out of Plex everywhere.

The sign-in shows up as “WhatsOn” under Plex → Settings → Authorized Devices, where you can remove it. Tokens stay on the WhatsOn server and never reach this browser.

Jellyfinin use

Use Jellyfin for the guide and library, or just let the Remote tab control Jellyfin apps playing on your TVs (seek bar, pause, next), including third-party apps that allow remote control.

Switching reloads the guide and library from the other server and clears your favorite channels, because the other server's channels are different.
