# NNNN. Short title that states the decision

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or pressure forced a choice? Facts only, and short enough to read in a minute.

## Decision

What we will do, in the active voice. Someone should be able to tell from this section whether a change follows it.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we now have to keep doing. Include the bad parts.

## Alternatives

What else was weighed, and why it lost. If nothing else was seriously weighed, say so.

## Where it lives

The code, tests and docs that carry this out, and any related ADRs.
